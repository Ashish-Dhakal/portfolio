Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026 · Dhakal Appify

This Privacy Policy explains how Dhakal Appify (“we”, “us”) handles information in the mobile game 2048 (package com.dhakalappify.game2048). The game is designed to work offline and does not require an account.

1. Information we store on your device

Gameplay data is saved locally on your device using on-device storage (for example SharedPreferences). This may include:

  • Saved and in-progress games
  • Scores, statistics, and high scores
  • Achievement progress and unlocked themes
  • Daily challenge completion state
  • Settings such as board size, theme, sound, and haptics
  • Ad frequency preferences used to limit how often ads are shown

This information stays on your device. We do not operate a user account system and we do not sync this data to our own servers.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows advertisements through Google AdMob (banner, interstitial, rewarded, app open, and similar formats). Google and its partners may collect device and advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, interaction data with ads, and diagnostic information needed to deliver, measure, and secure ads.

Where required (for example under GDPR/UMP), we request advertising consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change consent choices through system or in-app consent controls when available.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob & privacy.

3. Fonts and network use

The app may load fonts from Google Fonts when a network connection is available. If fonts cannot be fetched, the app falls back to system fonts. Other network traffic is primarily related to loading ads.

4. Children’s privacy

The app is a casual puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through ads or related services, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

5. Data retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the app’s storage, uninstall the app, or reset progress within the app (if such an option is available). Uninstalling removes locally stored game data. Advertising partners retain data according to their own policies.

6. Your choices

  • Limit or reset advertising ID in your device settings
  • Respond to consent prompts where shown
  • Clear app data or uninstall the app to remove local progress
  • Use a network firewall / offline mode (ads may not load)

7. Third-party services

We rely on third parties that process data under their own policies, including Google (AdMob, Play services, and related SDKs). We do not control those services beyond configuring the ad units used in the app.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Effective date” at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
