Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026. This policy explains what information Arrow Puzzle collects and how it is used.

Overview

Arrow Puzzle is a casual logic puzzle game for Android and iOS. Most gameplay works offline. We do not require you to create an account to play.

Information We Collect

We store game data locally on your device, including level progress, coins, hints, settings, daily challenge progress, purchase entitlement flags, and basic play statistics. This data stays on your device unless you back up or transfer the device through your platform’s normal backup features.

The app may show ads through Google AdMob. Ad networks may collect device identifiers, IP address, device/OS/app details, approximate location derived from IP, and ad interaction data for serving, measurement, and fraud prevention.

If you buy in-app products, payment is handled by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your full payment card details. We may receive purchase status, product identifiers, and entitlement information needed to unlock content.

If analytics or crash reporting is enabled, we or our providers may collect app usage events, technical device information, and crash or performance diagnostics. If you contact support, we receive the information you include in your message.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide and improve gameplay, save progress, deliver and measure ads, honor Remove Ads entitlements, process and restore purchases, diagnose bugs, respond to support requests, and comply with law and platform requirements.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with Google (AdMob and related advertising), Apple and Google Play (billing), analytics or crash providers if enabled, service providers who help operate the app, and authorities when required by law.

Children’s Privacy

Arrow Puzzle is a general-audience puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of selling it. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child provided personal information to us directly, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Your Choices

You can manage ad personalization through device settings and any in-app consent form we display where required. Remove Ads may be available as an in-app purchase. Uninstalling the app typically deletes on-device game data unless restored from a device backup. Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to legal limits.

Data Retention

On-device game data remains until you clear app data, uninstall, or reset progress. Support emails are retained as needed to respond and for legitimate business records. Ad and analytics providers retain data according to their policies.
