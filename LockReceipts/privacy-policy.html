Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026. This policy explains what information Lock Receipts (com.lockreceipts.app) collects and how it is used. The app is operated by Ashish Dhakal.

Overview

Lock Receipts is a personal daily-stamp Android app. It prints a cafe-style receipt of a countdown, habit streak, or bedtime plan onto a home-screen widget. There is no account, no login, and no cloud sync. Receipt data stays on your phone unless you choose to share an image.

Data Stored on Your Device

We do not run a server for your receipts. The app saves receipt setup, daily stamp history, optional profile name and photo, reminder settings, ad-consent flag, unlocked slots, share counts, points, themes, stamp inks, and streak tools in local storage on your phone.

Uninstalling the app deletes this local data. We cannot restore it. The home-screen widget shows a picture generated on the device; we do not upload that picture.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts, microphone, health data, or payment card details. Optional display name and gallery photo stay on the device only. This version has no in-app purchases.

Permissions

Internet is used to load ads and the consent form. Notifications and boot-completed access support the optional daily reminder. Photos/gallery access is used only when you pick an avatar, to copy one image into the app’s private files.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob (banner on history, interstitial after a stamp capped once per day, and optional rewarded ads). Ads are never placed inside the home-screen widget. Where required, we use Google’s User Messaging Platform for consent. You can change that choice later in Settings → Privacy options when shown.

Google may process advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad diagnostic data. We do not sell your personal data and do not receive your receipt contents from Google. See Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Sharing, Notifications & Choices

If you share a receipt, the app builds a PNG and opens the Android share sheet. The destination app receives only that image under its own policies. Reminders are local notifications; we do not use a push server.

You can turn reminders off, withdraw ad consent where available, limit ad tracking in Android settings, avoid sharing images, or uninstall the app to delete local data.

Children

Lock Receipts is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher age required in your country) and is not part of Google Play’s Designed for Families program. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
